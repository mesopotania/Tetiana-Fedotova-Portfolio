Info & Contact

Portrait and black & white photographer.

Hi, I'm Tetiana. I photograph people, mostly in natural, low light and in their own spaces, with enough time for them to settle in and forget about the camera. I'm interested in quiet moments and honest expressions more than perfect poses.

Alongside portrait sessions I keep an ongoing black & white series. I'm open to personal portraits, editorial work and collaborations. Write to me and tell me what you have in mind.
